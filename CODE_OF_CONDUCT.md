# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in dunlin to be free of harassment for everyone,
whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and respectful. Technical disagreement is welcome, personal
  attacks are not.
- Assume good faith and ask before assuming intent.
- Keep discussion on the problem, the code and the evidence.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks,
publishing someone's private information, and repeatedly derailing technical
discussion.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to emresimsek2013@gmail.com. Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban
anyone whose behaviour they find unacceptable, and will explain why.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
